Chapter 7: The Trap

Sunday, Dawn

Eszter wakes up. She doesn't remember falling asleep. The small room at the Királyrét forester's house is cold. Outside, the birds are already singing. The sky lightens at six. Börzsöny doesn't sleep. Never.

Neither does Péter.

Last night the letter. The threat. Meet me where it all began. April 28, midnight. Alone, Eszter.

Today is April 28. Twenty-five years after Mária's death. The same day. Péter didn't choose this date by accident. With him everything is symbolic. Everything is ritual.

The phone vibrates on the pillow. Unknown number. Eszter answers.

"Kovács."

Female voice. Trembling. Desperate.

"Eszter? Captain Székely gave me your number. I... I'm Varga Szilvia."

Eszter sits up on the bed. Szilvia. From the safe house. The psychologist. The last living witness.

"What happened?"

Szilvia's voice breaks.

"She died. Péter killed her."

Eszter's heart stops. Impossible. The safe house. The police. No...

"Who died?" she asks quietly.

"My friend. Karolina. She was with me last night. This morning... they found her. In the garden. The garden of the safe house."

Eszter closes her eyes. Péter got there. Somehow. To the safe house. And killed someone.

"How...?"

"I don't know," Szilvia sobs. "The police say he climbed over the fence. At night. Karolina went out at ten to smoke. And... she didn't come back. At dawn she was lying there. In the garden. Blue paint on her wrist. And next to her... a photo. The same photo. And a letter. For me. 'Szilvia, if Eszter doesn't come, more will die.'"

Eszter jumps out of bed. Opens the door. The hallway is empty. Gábor is still sleeping somewhere. Everyone is sleeping.

"Listen to me, Szilvia," she says firmly. "This isn't your fault. Karolina... was in the wrong place at the wrong time. But you're safe. Understand? Péter wants me. Not others. And I... I'm meeting him today. And I'll end this."

"But he... he..."

"I know," Eszter cuts in. "I know what he wants. He wants me to go to him. Alone. And I will go."

"This is madness," Szilvia whispers. "He'll kill you."

"Maybe. But if I don't go... nobody will be safe. Not you either."

She hangs up the phone. Goes back to the room. The phone still in her hand. She calls Székely.

András Székely — Sunday Morning, Seven O'Clock

Székely hasn't slept yet. He's been awake all night. Waiting for the call. He knew it would come.

"Eszter," he says when he answers. "What happened?"

"Péter killed someone at Szilvia's safe house. Karolina, Szilvia's friend. He climbed over the fence at night. Marked her with blue paint. And left a letter: 'If Eszter doesn't come, more will die.'"

Székely closes his eyes.

"This is a trap. Don't go alone."

"I know it's a trap. But this is the only way. If I don't go... Péter won't stop. After Karolina... anyone could be next. Anyone. Randomly. Until I..."

"Until you go to him."

"Yes."

"Eszter, this is suicide."

Silence. Then Eszter quietly:

"I have to. For Anna. Alone."

Székely sighs. He knows he can't stop her. Eszter has already decided. Twenty-five years ago, when Anna disappeared, Eszter decided: if she ever learns the truth, she won't let go at any cost.

"All right," he finally says. "But Gábor will follow you. From a distance. And mountain rescue will be on standby. If anything goes wrong..."

"I know."

"And Eszter..." Székely's voice changes. "Take care of yourself."

"I'll try."

Sunday, Eight O'Clock — Királyrét, Forester's House

Gábor sits on the edge of Eszter's bed. Smoking a cigarette. Eszter doesn't say anything about it. Eszter is already dressed. Hiking clothes, boots, backpack. The pistol at her belt.

"I spoke with Edit," Gábor says quietly. "She asked you not to go."

"I know."

"I spoke with Székely too. He thinks it's a trap."

"I know."

"And you're still going."

"Yes."

Gábor glances at her. Concern in his eyes. But not anger. Just... acceptance.

"Then I'm going too. Fifty meters behind you. If anything happens..."

"I know," Eszter says. She takes Gábor's hand. "Thank you."

Gábor nods.

"Anna was lucky to have a sister like you."

Eszter turns her head. She doesn't want to cry. Not now. Not today.

"Anna tried to save Mária," she says quietly. "And I... I couldn't save Anna."

"But now you can," Gábor says. "Now you can serve justice for her."

Flashback — Saturday Night, Twelve O'Clock

Szilvia sat in the safe house living room. Next to Karolina. They drank coffee. Talked.

"Thank you for coming," Szilvia said quietly. "I know it's... dangerous."

"Don't worry," Karolina said smiling. "The police are here. We're safe."

But Szilvia didn't feel safe. Péter's letters arrived every day. Texts. Emails. Always the same: "Do you remember Mária?" "We'll meet soon." "After twenty-five years."

Szilvia knew. Péter always finds a way.

At ten, Karolina went out to smoke in the garden. Szilvia stayed inside. Watched a movie. Tried not to think.

At eleven, Karolina still hadn't come back. Szilvia went out. The garden was empty. Dark. The fence was high, steel.

But on the other side, in the shadows... Szilvia saw. A figure stood. Motionless. Watching her.

Szilvia ran back to the house. Locked the door. Called the police.

But it was too late.

In the morning they found Karolina. In the corner of the garden. Blue paint on her wrist. Photo next to her. And a letter.

Szilvia read the letter. And knew.

Péter doesn't want her. Péter wants Eszter. And until Eszter comes... anyone can die.

Sunday, Nine O'Clock — Edge of the Forest

Eszter stands alone in the clearing. The map in her lap. Zoltán, the young forester, showed her where the house is. Péter's house. Where Péter has lived for five years. Alone. Deep in the forest.

Five kilometers. Steep climb. Narrow path. And then... the house.

Gábor is fifty meters behind her. Eszter sees him watching. Eszter nods. Gábor waves back. Take care of yourself.

Eszter starts walking.

The Forest

The forest is silent. The trees dense. Beeches, oaks, poplars. On the ground moss and leaf litter. The air is cool but not cold. Spring day. Beautiful. As if nothing bad exists.

But Eszter knows. All bad exists. And it's all here. In this forest. Twenty steps from her. Péter.

Eszter follows the blue markers. From one tree to another. Péter's trail markers. Péter's world.

In the first twenty minutes the ground is a gentle slope. Eszter proceeds on the path. But her thoughts run to Anna. Always to her.

Anna always loved the forest. Even in their childhood. When their father took them hiking in Pilis. Anna would rush ahead. Eszter followed. Always followed.

Eszter couldn't protect her even then.

Among the trees the light breaks. Shadows dance in the leaf litter. Eszter stops. Listens.

Nothing. Only the wind.

Eszter continues.

Her thoughts sink deeper. Anna and Mária. In 2009 Anna would have come back. She wanted to confess. But Péter... Péter didn't let her. Because Péter wanted justice. But not the court's justice. His own.

After thirty minutes the slope becomes steep. Eszter's breathing becomes heavier. Her ankle hurts. Old injury. Twenty years ago, from an accident. But she doesn't stop. Not now.

The trees become denser. The markers farther apart. Péter doesn't want an easy path. Péter is testing. Eszter knows.

Eszter stops under a beech tree. Drinks water from the canteen. Turns around. Gábor nowhere. But Eszter knows he's there. Somewhere. Following.

Edit called last night. Asked her not to go. Said this is suicide. Eszter listened. Didn't say what she thinks. That Anna has been dead for twenty-five years. That there's been no justice for twenty-five years. That for twenty-five years Eszter wakes every morning with: Where are you, Anna? Where are you?

And now... now the answer is ahead. In Péter's house. Deep in the forest.

Eszter continues.

After forty-five minutes the path becomes narrower. The trees almost touch from the sides. The sun barely filters through. Eszter slows. Her hand on the pistol.

The air changes. Colder. Damper. Somewhere nearby a stream babbles.

Eszter follows the sound. After five minutes she reaches the stream. Narrow, crystal clear. Squeezed between rocks.

On the other side of the stream... a marker. Blue. Péter's marker.

Eszter crosses the stream. The rocks are slippery. She almost falls. But catches herself.

Continues.

And then... she stops.

Ahead is a clearing. And at the far end of the clearing...

A house.

The Clearing

Eszter watches the house from among the trees. Small wooden house. Green roof. Mossy beams. The door closed. The window dark. Silence.

But smoke rises from the chimney.

Péter is home.

Eszter takes a deep breath. Her hand on the pistol. Her heart beats fast. But not from fear. Rather... from anticipation.

Twenty-five years. And now...

Eszter steps into the clearing.

The grass is knee-high. Dew glistens on it. The sun is already high. Must be ten o'clock.

Eszter walks through the grass. Slowly. Carefully.

Twenty meters to the house.

Ten meters.

Five.

Eszter stops ten meters before the door. Looks at the house. Listens to the silence.

No sound. Only the wind. And somewhere far away... a bird singing.

Eszter steps forward. Slow steps. Cautious.

Five meters from the door.

Four.

Three.

Two.

The door opens.

Péter stands on the threshold.

Péter

Tall. Broad-shouldered. Forester clothes. Brown coat. His face wrinkled, as if carved from tree bark. His hair gray. His eyes dark.

But there's no anger in them.

Only... tiredness.

"Eszter," he says quietly. "You came."

Eszter doesn't take her hand off the pistol.

"Péter. Horváth Péter."

Péter slowly nods.

"Haven't heard that name in a long time. Here everyone just calls me 'the forester.' Or nobody calls me anything. Because I'm already nobody."

"You're a murderer," Eszter says.

Péter's eyes change. Soften. Open. And Eszter sees the pain in them. Twenty-five years of pain.

"Yes," Péter says simply. "I am. But you know... why."

"Mária."

Péter's eyes tremble. Change.

"Mária," he whispers. "Dead for twenty-five years. And every day... every day I see her face."

Silence. Péter steps back.

"Come in, Eszter. I'll tell you everything. Twenty-five years. Every night. Every day. Every minute when Mária... when Mária wasn't with me."

Eszter hesitates. She knows it's a trap. She knows it's dangerous.

But she also knows: Anna has been waiting for the truth for twenty-five years.

Eszter steps forward. Crosses the threshold.

Péter closes the door behind her.

The House

Inside is semi-darkness. Candlelight. The single room is small and oppressive.

And then Eszter sees.

Mária's photos. Hundreds of them. Everywhere. On the walls. On the table. On the floor. Hung on wires under the ceiling. Mária's face. Everywhere Mária's face.

Eszter's heart constricts.

In the center of the table... an altar.

Mária's largest photo. Candles around it. Twelve. All burning. Blue scarf at the edge of the altar. Hiking boots. Silver necklace. And flowers. Dried flowers. Twenty-five-year-old flowers.

"I picked these there," Péter says quietly behind her. "At that place. Where Mária... where she died."

Eszter looks around. On the walls not just photos. There are letters too. In Péter's writing. All addressed to Mária. Dear Mária. Today twenty-five years have passed. Today it's April 28 again. Today it's raining again. And you... you're still not here.

Eszter looks at Péter.

"You've lived like this for twenty-five years?"

Péter nods.

"Twenty-five years. Alone. With Mária's memory."

Eszter slowly walks around the room. Looks at the photos. Mária smiling. Smiling everywhere. Young. Beautiful. Twenty years old.

"You loved her," Eszter says quietly.

"I loved her," Péter says. "More than my life. And they... they took her from me."

"Who?"

"Balázs. Szilvia. Viktor. All of them. That night. April 28. There was a storm. Mária wanted to go out. I... I wasn't there. I was in Budapest, had work. But Mária... Mária went out alone. And they... they wanted to hold her back. Balázs grabbed her. Szilvia yelled at her. Viktor locked the door."

"They wanted to save her," Eszter says.

"They wanted to kill her," Péter says quietly. "Because Mária wasn't crazy. Mária knew what she was doing. Mária didn't want to live anymore. And they... they wouldn't let her die. Wouldn't give her dignity. They pulled her back. Humiliated her. And then... then Mária went out anyway. Alone. Into the storm. And died. But not with dignity. Running. Fleeing. Like an animal."

Eszter listens. Péter's voice trembles.

"And Anna," Péter says. "Anna knew. Anna saw. Anna tried to help Mária. But it was too late. And in 2009... Anna wanted to come back. She wanted to confess. But I... I didn't let her."

"You killed her," Eszter says quietly.

Péter closes his eyes.

"I didn't want to. But Anna didn't understand. Anna thought the law would bring justice. But the law... the law is just paper. Case number. Court verdict. I... I wanted real justice."

"Revenge," Eszter says.

Péter opens his eyes. Looks into Eszter's eyes.

"Yes," he says simply. "Revenge."

Silence. The candles flicker.

Eszter sits down. Péter too.

And in the candlelight, in the silent house, in the heart of the forest...

Péter begins to speak.
